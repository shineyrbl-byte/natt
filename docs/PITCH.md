# 3-minute pitch script

**0:00 – Problem (30 s)**
"This is Awa. She sells fabric at Marché HLM in Dakar and she's in a *natt*,
a tontine, with 12 other sellers. Every week the organizer walks around with
cash and a notebook. Cash gets lost, people argue about who paid, and
sometimes the organizer disappears with the pot. Millions of people in West
Africa save this way."

**0:30 – Solution (30 s)**
"Natt keeps the tontine people already trust and removes the weak points.
Members pay from their phone with Lightning, the payout order is drawn fairly,
and every payment is a signed receipt on Nostr that nobody can edit."

**1:00 – Live demo (90 s)**
1. Show the group screen in French: round 1, Fatou receives, 3 of 5 paid.
2. As Aminata: tap Pay, show the QR code, pay. The bar moves to 4/5.
3. Switch language to English with one tap.
4. As Fatou: everyone has paid, so paste an invoice and tap Receive the pot. Round 2 opens.
5. Open the public ledger: signed receipts and a ✓ "nothing changed".
6. Ask Natt Helper "Who hasn't paid?"

**2:30 – Impact and next steps (30 s)**
"One organizer runs 10 to 20 members. Ten organizers at one market means
about 200 people and 200 Lightning payments a week. Next we reduce custody
with hold invoices, add Nostr login and WhatsApp reminders, and pilot at
Marché HLM. Natt: the tontine you know, without the cash and without the
doubt. Jërëjëf."
